# Expent Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Expent app ("Expent", "we", "us") collects, how we
use it, and the choices you have. Expent is published by **[DEVELOPER NAME]**. You can reach us at
**[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

- Expent is a **manual** money tracker. It **does not connect to your bank**, read your SMS, or ask for
  bank account numbers, card numbers, UPI IDs or passwords.
- The only financial information we have is what **you type into the app**: your transactions, the
  names you give your accounts and categories, your income, EMIs, subscriptions and budgets.
- From your Google account we receive only your **name and email address**.
- We **don't sell** your data or use it for advertising. Expent has no ads.
- You can **delete your account and all your data** from inside the app at any time.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us
| What | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Account details** (from Google Sign-In) | Your name and email address | To create your account and sign you in |
| **Money entries you create** | Transactions (amount, date, type, notes), category names, account and card *names* you choose (e.g. "SBI Savings"), income sources, EMIs and loans, subscriptions, budgets | To show your spending, cashflow, reminders and history: the core of the app |
| **Feedback** | Star rating and any message you send from "Help us improve" | To improve Expent |

Expent never asks for account numbers, card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, net-banking credentials or
government IDs, and you shouldn't enter them anywhere in the app.

### Information collected automatically
| What | Why | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| **Crash reports and diagnostics** (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces) | To find and fix crashes | Firebase Crashlytics (Google) |
| **App usage events** (e.g. screens opened, features used) and an app-instance ID | To understand which features are used and improve them | Firebase Analytics (Google) |
| **Push notification token** | To send reminders and announcements to your device | Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) |

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, SMS, call logs, or files.

## 2. AI-assisted entry

If you use **AI entry** ("Spent $25 on pizza yesterday using cash"), the sentence you type, along with
your category and account names, is sent to **Google's Gemini API** so it can work out the amount,
category, account and date. It's used only to process that request. Use manual entry if you prefer
not to use this feature.

## 3. How we use information

We use your information only to:
- provide the app: store and sync your entries, calculate totals, show reminders;
- keep your account secure and signed in;
- send notifications you've allowed (reminders, important announcements);
- fix crashes and improve features;
- reply when you contact us.

We **do not** sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to build advertising
profiles.

## 4. Who we share information with

Only with service providers that help us run Expent, and only for that purpose:
- **Google** (Google Sign-In, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging and
  the Gemini API);
- our **hosting and database providers**, which store your account and entries on secure servers;
- **Cloudflare**, which delivers the app's settings and text (this contains no personal data).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users
or of Expent.

## 5. Where your data is stored and how it's protected

- Your entries are stored **on your phone** (so the app works offline) and on **our servers** (so they
  sync and survive a reinstall).
- All data sent between the app and our servers is **encrypted in transit (HTTPS)**.
- Sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in **encrypted storage**.
- Access to our servers and database is restricted.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix issues quickly.

## 6. How long we keep data

- We keep your account and entries **for as long as your account exists**.
- When you delete your account, your account and all your entries are **permanently deleted from our
  database immediately**, and the app erases the copy on your phone.
- Crash reports and analytics are kept by Google for a limited period under its standard retention
  settings, and aren't linked to your name or email.

## 7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:
- **In the app:** Profile → **Delete account** → type DELETE → confirm. This deletes your account and
  all your data (transactions, accounts, categories, income, EMIs, subscriptions, budgets, settings and
  feedback). It can't be undone.
- **Without the app:** email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** from the email address on your account, with the
  subject "Delete my Expent account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Signing in again later creates a new, empty account.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction:** you can view and edit all your entries in the app.
- **Deletion:** see section 7.
- **Notifications:** turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
- **Google account access:** you can also remove Expent's access from your Google Account settings
  (Security → Third-party connections).

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or
the EU/UK GDPR), you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your data or objecting to
processing. Email us and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

## 9. Children

Expent is meant for adults managing their own money and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the
minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect children's data. If you think a child has
given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you
know in the app. Continuing to use Expent after a change means you accept the updated policy.
